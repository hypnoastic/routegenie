Privacy Policy

Route Genie helps users plan routes, trips, and navigation with voice-first AI features.

What we collect

  • Account details such as name and email when you sign in.
  • Trip searches, saved trips, and route preferences you choose to store.
  • Voice and route request data needed to process navigation requests.

How we use data

  • To sign you in and maintain your account session.
  • To generate routes, smart stops, and trip suggestions.
  • To save trip history and personalize future route recommendations.

Sharing

Route Genie uses Google services for maps, places, and authentication, and infrastructure providers for hosting and database storage. We do not sell personal information.
